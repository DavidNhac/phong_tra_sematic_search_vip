      The most famous Acoustic cafes in Hanoi

      Hà Nội, Việt Nam
      No noise is as hot as Saigon, Hanoi has a romantic and classic look. Therefore, cultural and artistic activities here also have their own style. After a tiring day of work, there is nothing better than sipping a cup of coffee with melodious Acoustic music.

      Acoustic is not picky about the audience, it is suitable for many classes from office people, students, students to middle-aged people. Acoustics can be played anywhere, even on the sidewalk, such as street music groups. Grasping the needs and tastes of customers, many cafes have shaped their own acoustic style. Each acoustic cafe has a style, a different musical personality, do not mix, but all have in common that every time the music plays, the audience will no longer feel the distance between the players. playing with listeners, who seem to be immersed in authentic music. To help you choose the ideal cafe, Tatinta will introduce you to the most famous Acoustic cafes in Hanoi.

      ABC cafe

      ABC cafe is located in lane 15 Ta Quang Buu, quite familiar to students in the area of Polytechnic School and University of Civil Engineering. The shop is decorated in deep tones that are embellished with orange-red lights. The space of the shop is not too big and is decorated with photos of old Hanoi, creating a feeling of closeness and idyllic. Acoustic performances here take place in the form of musical exchanges. In addition to the opportunity to enjoy great voices, you also have the ability to show your talent.

      ABC Cafe
      Address: 6 Alley 40 Ta Quang Buu, Hai Ba Trung District, Hanoi
      Opening hours: 09:00 – 23:00
      Price: 15,000 – 30,000 VND
      Fanpage: ABC Cafe (Acoustic Ballad Cafe)

      Acoustic Café

      Acoustic Cafe is located in the Old Quarter area, a familiar address to many Hanoians. The shop is decorated in a rustic style, as simple as its name. Since its presence, the shop has brought Ha Thanh people a great music listening space. The orchestra is invested quite carefully to create gentle and attractive music. Here, Acoustic music performances take place almost every night. The shop is only open in the evening, you can come any day of the week to enjoy the familiar music you love.

      Acoustic Café
      Address: 7 Nguyen Sieu, Hoan Kiem District, Hanoi
      Opening hours: 19:30 - 23:30
      Price: 100,000 – 440,000 VND

      G4U cafe

      G4U cafe stands out with the peaceful space of houses in the Northern countryside, decorated with baskets, fish baskets, and bamboo skillfully to create a special style. G4U is impressed by the quality Acoustic music. Each night, the bar will bring to listeners different styles of music performance, creating a sense of excitement for the audience. The shop has two main areas, one is on the porch if you like the coolness or the cozy space in the house.

      Cafe G4U
      Address: No. 29 Nguyen Khang, Cau Giay District, Hanoi
      Opening hours: 07:00 – 23:00
      Price: 20,000 – 55,000 VND

      Trinh Ca Cafe

      Trinh Ca Cafe is located in a small alley on To Hieu Street, decorated with rustic simplicity with wooden tables and chairs, bamboo and rattan, and famous paintings of musician Trinh Cong Son. This is where you can immerse yourself in the space of Trinh music. The shop has a quiet space, quite suitable for those who like nostalgia and peace. On every corner of the shop are recorded traces of time. Trinh Ca Cafe's menu has many dishes with names reminiscent of Trinh's music such as: Impermanent coffee, Love Sorrow, Sea Me mocktail, Pink Rain, Diem Xua, Thu Phai, Sunny Glass, Call the Four Seasons.

      Trinh Ca Cafe
      Address: lane 233 To Hieu, Cau Giay district, Hanoi
      Opening hours: 07:00 – 23:00
      Price: 20,000 – 55,000 VND

      Typograf Live Music Cafe

      Different from other Acoustic bars, Typograf gives people a space that is both classic and modern. Each table, chair, or small items are arranged quite meticulously but no less delicately. The cozy space with wood-grained walls is accentuated by the green trees under the yellow light, creating more romance. The shop organizes Acoustic music nights every day except Mondays. This is a place where you can enjoy a variety of music, from upbeat songs to sweet, deep music that will bring you an enjoyable evening.

      Typograf Live Music Cafe
      Address: No. 158 Thuong Dinh, Thanh Xuan District, Hanoi
      Opening hours: 09:00 – 23:00
      Price: 50,000 – 100,000 VND

      Coffee hamlet

      Xom cafe is a classic Hanoi cafe that is very familiar to young people in Hanoi. The shop has a spacious space, divided into two areas: indoor and outdoor. Xom cafe organizes Acoustic and Trinh music nights every Friday and Saturday night. After a tiring working week, being released to the music will help the mood to be more relaxed. The big plus point for the restaurant is that the menu not only has delicious drinks but also extremely cheap prices with enthusiastic and attentive service style.

      Coffee hamlet
      Address 1: 111C8 To Hieu, Cau Giay, Hanoi
      Address 2: N6E Trung Hoa, Nhan Chinh, Thanh Xuan, Hanoi
      Opening hours 06:30 - 23:00
      Price: 12,000 - 32,000 VND

      Like Cafe

      Like cafe is a cafe quite familiar with young people in Hanoi. The shop is built in the architectural style of an old villa decorated with deep brown tones and airy garden space. Acousitic music is performed here every evening. The lightness, combined with the space of the shop, gives people a feeling of comfort and freedom. In particular, there are many young singers here, where the audience can come to interact with their idols.

      Like coffee
      Address: 10 Khuc Hao, Ba Dinh District, Hanoi
      Phone: (024) 62 971 979
      Hours: 07:00 – 23:00
      Price: 20,000 – 55,000 VND

      Giang Coffee

      Students from the Academy of Security, Architecture or Hanoi University are familiar with Giang Coffee. The space of the restaurant has a modern and youthful style decorated by oil paintings combined with the orange-red light of the lamps to create a warm feeling. Acoustic music nights here take place in a new style. Audiences can enjoy many different genres of music, from Vietnamese songs to exciting international ones.

      Giang Coffee
      Address: 119B Tran Phu, Ha Dong District, Hanoi
      Opening hours: 07:00 – 23:00
      Price: 25,000 – 66,000 VND

      Swing Music Lounge

      Located right in the heart of Hanoi city, Swing Music Lounge is a famous cafe that everyone knows. The space is designed in a unique and strange architectural style, inspired by French houses, creating an attraction for everyone. Swing Music Lounge regularly organizes Acoustic music nights. In particular, many music nights have the participation of many famous singers such as My Linh, Tuan Hung, ...

      Swing Music Lounge
      Address: 3rd floor, 21 Trang Tien, Hoan Kiem, Hanoi
      Price: 50,000 VND – 150,000 VND

      The most famous Acoustic cafes in Hanoi

      No noise is as hot as Saigon, Hanoi has a romantic and classic look. Therefore, cultural and artistic activities here also have their own style. After a tiring day of work, there is nothing better than sipping a cup of coffee with melodious Acoustic music.

      Nostalgic cafes in Hanoi

      It is impossible to count the number of coffee shops in Hanoi because in this 1,000-year-old city, coffee has become a distinct culture. Not only sipping a cup of hot coffee, Hanoians also enjoy the space attached to that cafe. Amidst the hustle and bustle of Hanoi, you can still find peaceful corners inside nostalgic cafes.

      Serene Cafe & Lounge

      Serein Café & Lounge is an emerging cafe for young people in Hanoi. What is unique about this cafe is the perfect view of the train tracks. This is the perfect place to sip cocktails and enjoy the unspoiled scenery of Long Bien Bridge

      Ta Hien Street

      Ta Hien Street, located in the heart of Hanoi’s Old Quarter, is a famous “international crossroads” that attracts a large number of domestic and foreign tourists. With an ancient space mixed with a touch of modernity, Ta Hien is a culinary and nightlife paradise, featuring cool draft beer, attractive street food and a bustling atmosphere. This is an ideal destination to experience the culture and vibrant life of Hanoi.
